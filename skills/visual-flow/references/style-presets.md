# Style Presets

Detailed color palettes, typography, and design language for each supported style. Read this file when you need the specific values for a style preset.

## Nano Banana (default)

The signature style. Think Linear, Vercel, or Stripe marketing pages adapted for infographics.

**Background:** Deep navy or near-black (#0F172A or #1E1B4B)
**Primary palette:**
- Blue: #3B82F6 (primary actions, first stages)
- Green: #22C55E (success, completion stages)
- Amber: #F59E0B (warnings, optional elements, quality gates)
- Purple: #A855F7 (configuration, setup, meta-stages)
- Rose: #F43F5E (critical paths, blockers, alerts)
- Cyan: #06B6D4 (integrations, external connections)

**Typography:**
- Font: Inter, Poppins, or similar geometric sans-serif
- Title: 28-36px, Bold, white (#F8FAFC)
- Section headers: 18-22px, Semibold, white
- Body text: 13-16px, Medium, light gray (#CBD5E1)
- Captions: 11-13px, Regular, muted gray (#64748B)

**Design elements:**
- Rounded rectangles (12-16px radius) for content blocks
- Subtle drop shadows (0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.3))
- Solid thick arrows (3-4px) for required flow, white or light gray
- Dashed arrows (2px) for optional paths
- Generous whitespace between elements
- No gradients — flat solid colors only
- Thin borders (1px) in slightly lighter shade of background for subtle separation

**Badges/tags:** Pill-shaped, small, using component's color at 20% opacity with full-color text

## Minimal

Clean, airy, maximum whitespace. Think Apple product pages.

**Background:** Pure white (#FFFFFF) or very light gray (#FAFAFA)
**Primary palette:**
- Charcoal: #1F2937 (primary text and borders)
- Medium gray: #6B7280 (secondary text)
- Light gray: #E5E7EB (borders, dividers)
- Single accent: #2563EB (one blue for highlights and active states)

**Typography:**
- Font: SF Pro, Helvetica Neue, or similar neutral sans-serif
- Title: 32px, Light weight, charcoal
- Section headers: 18px, Medium, charcoal
- Body: 14px, Regular, medium gray
- Captions: 12px, Regular, light gray

**Design elements:**
- Thin 1px borders instead of filled backgrounds
- Hairline arrows (1-2px) in medium gray
- Extreme whitespace — let elements breathe
- No shadows, no fills — line art aesthetic
- Circles for nodes, straight lines for connections

## Corporate

Polished and professional. Think McKinsey slide or annual report.

**Background:** White (#FFFFFF) with structured grid
**Primary palette:**
- Navy: #1E3A5F (primary headers, key elements)
- Steel blue: #4A7C9B (secondary elements)
- Gold: #C4952A (accents, highlights, callouts)
- Light blue: #E8F0FE (background fills for content blocks)
- Charcoal: #333333 (body text)

**Typography:**
- Font: Georgia, Merriweather, or similar serif for headers; Arial/Helvetica for body
- Title: 28px, Bold, navy
- Section headers: 18px, Bold, navy
- Body: 14px, Regular, charcoal
- Captions: 11px, Italic, steel blue

**Design elements:**
- Sharp corners (2-4px radius max)
- Structured grid layout with clear alignment
- Thick bottom borders on headers (3px gold)
- Numbered steps with navy circles and white numbers
- Professional iconography — briefcase, chart, handshake style
- Subtle background pattern or grid lines

---

## Playful

Energetic and approachable. Think Notion or Figma marketing.

**Background:** Warm off-white (#FFF7ED) or soft cream (#FFFBEB)
**Primary palette:**
- Coral: #FB7185 (primary actions)
- Violet: #8B5CF6 (secondary elements)
- Teal: #14B8A6 (success, data)
- Amber: #FBBF24 (highlights, badges)
- Indigo: #6366F1 (links, interactive)

**Typography:**
- Font: Nunito, Quicksand, or similar rounded sans-serif
- Title: 32px, ExtraBold, dark text (#1C1917)
- Section headers: 20px, Bold, dark text
- Body: 15px, Medium, warm gray (#57534E)
- Captions: 12px, Regular, warm gray

**Design elements:**
- Very rounded corners (16-24px radius)
- Soft colored shadows matching component color at 15% opacity
- Wavy or organic connector lines instead of straight arrows
- Emoji-style icons or hand-drawn illustration style
- Slightly rotated elements (1-2 degrees) for casual feel
- Sticker/badge aesthetic for tags and labels

---

## Blueprint

Technical and precise. Think engineering diagrams or API documentation.

**Background:** Dark blue-gray (#1A1D23) or #0D1117 (GitHub dark)
**Primary palette:**
- Cyan: #00D4FF (primary lines, active elements)
- Green: #00FF88 (success, data flow)
- Orange: #FF8800 (warnings, configuration)
- White: #E6EDF3 (text, borders)
- Muted blue: #30363D (subtle backgrounds, panels)

**Typography:**
- Font: JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, or similar monospace
- Title: 24px, Bold, white
- Section headers: 16px, Bold, cyan
- Body: 13px, Regular, light gray (#8B949E)
- Captions: 11px, Regular, muted (#484F58)

**Design elements:**
- Fine grid overlay on background (subtle, ~5% opacity)
- Sharp corners (0-4px radius)
- 1px solid borders in muted blue
- Arrows with explicit direction markers (triangular heads)
- Dotted grid alignment guides
- Terminal/code-block aesthetic for detail panels
- Status dots (green/amber/red) for component health
